# DARK-MIRROR-AI-Honeypot-AI-decoy-Generator
Dark Mirror AI is an intelligent cybersecurity deception platform designed to detect, analyse, and study malicious activities through AI-powered honeypots and dynamic decoy generation. Traditional honeypots often rely on static configurations, predictable services, and predefined fake resources.
The system uses Artificial Intelligence and  analysis to generate convincing decoy systems, files, credentials, services, and network resources based on the targeted environment. Instead of simply waiting for attacks, Dark Mirror AI continuously observes attacker , analyses commands and interaction patterns, and modifies the decoy environment to maintain deception. Suspicious activities are recorded and analysed to identify attack techniques, intent, movement patterns, and potential security threats.

Traditional honeypots are static, rigid, and easy to fingerprint. They rely on pre-configured services, fixed banners, and hardcoded fake files that experienced attackers can quickly identify and bypass. This leads to three critical failures
Low engagement: attackers detect the honeypot within minutes and disengage, resulting in short session times and minimal telemetry.
Poor-quality intelligence: static honeypots capture generic commands and payloads that lack context, making it hard for soc teams to extract actionable threat intelligence.
No adaptation: when attackers use new  (tactics, techniques, procedures), traditional honeypots cannot evolve their deception strategy, leaving them ineffective against sophisticated or automated attacks.

Dark Mirror AI is a proactive, honeypot that uses generative AI to create dynamic, context-aware decoys that adapt in real time to attacker actions. Instead of serving the same fake SSH banner or API response to every connection, Dark Mirror AI:
Profiles attacker  (commands, payloads, request patterns) to classify intent and sophistication.
Generates realistic decoys on-the-fly using LLMs—fake files, API responses, logs, credentials, and even fake vulnerabilities tailored to the attacker's observed actions.

Adapts the deception surface by rotating banners, injecting delays, and modifying responses to avoid fingerprinting and prolong engagement.
Feeds high-fidelity telemetry back into a threat intelligence pipeline, enabling SOC teams to understand attacker goals, not just their tools.
